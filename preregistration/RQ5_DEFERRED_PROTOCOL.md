## RQ5

> Compared with unaided manual practice, does the structured refinement method
> reduce silent narrowing, omissions, orphan controls, and inconsistent gate
> assignments?

Answered by the committed follow-up study, not by this paper.

## Primary outcomes (frozen)

1. **Silent-Narrowing Rate (SNR)** — `SNᵢ = 𝟙[ Qᵢ* \ Qᵢ ≠ ∅ ∧ no explicit residual
   or non-runtime disposition covers the difference ]`; `SNR = Σᵢ SNᵢ / |R|`.
2. **Incorrect-gate rate.**
3. **Disposition Completeness (DC).**

All other outcomes are secondary. No conclusion will be based solely on an
uncorrected collection of exploratory comparisons.

## Secondary outcomes

Field-Level Derivation Fidelity `DFᵢ = |Fᵢ ∩ Fᵢ*| / |Fᵢ*|`, `DF` the mean over `R`;
omission rate; orphan-control rate; completion time; requested clarifications.

Threshold, operator, authority-tuple and unknown-handling fields require **exact
agreement** with the adjudicated reference. Semantic similarity alone is not
accepted.

## Reference standard (frozen)

An independent **three-person adjudication panel** with expertise spanning AI
governance, operational risk, and policy/control engineering. Panel members have
no authorship, ownership, or commercial interest in the method.

Procedure: each member first assesses every held-out row independently; then a
blinded, structured Delphi reconciliation. Disagreements and final rationales are
retained and published with the study data.

The author does not participate in adjudication and does not modify the held-out
register, catalog, or thresholds after panel review begins. **`Φ` output is not
treated as ground truth**; it is compared against the independently adjudicated
Approved Control Specifications.

## Design (frozen)

* **Participants:** at least 8 independent practitioners, planned maximum 12.
* **Instrument:** a held-out register of at least 30 risk rows across three
  domains, including at least one non-financial domain. Rows include
  runtime-compilable, non-runtime, ambiguous and deliberately incomplete examples.
* **Blocks:** two matched blocks A and B with comparable numbers of runtime
  controls, non-runtime controls, `C*` risks, non-`C*` risks, reason-code classes,
  and numeric, structural, lookup and attestation conditions.
* **Assignment:** randomized counterbalanced crossover. Half complete Block A
  manually and Block B using GC-IR/K; the other half the reverse. **No reviewer
  sees the same risk row in both conditions** — this reduces recall and training
  effects while permitting reviewer-level paired analysis.
* **Randomization seed:** frozen in `preregistration/TIER0_FREEZE.md` and not
  drawn until recruitment closes.

### Recorded per row

1. disposition; 2. reason code where applicable; 3. event type and observable;
4. evidence producer and source; 5. subject/action/resource/destination;
6. evaluation basis; 7. threshold contract or structural condition; 8. gate type,
gate source and mandatory role; 9. unknown handling; 10. escalation path.

Completion time and requested clarifications are recorded automatically.

## Analysis plan (frozen)

### Inter-rater reliability
Fleiss' κ for nominal multi-rater outcomes (disposition, reason code, gate source);
Cohen's kappa per reviewer against the reference, pooled descriptively; weighted
kappa for gate severity **only if** the ordering and weights are frozen before the
study. Krippendorff's α as a robustness statistic; Gwet's AC1 as a
prevalence-robust sensitivity analysis. **No qualitative label such as
"substantial" substitutes for the numerical estimate and its uncertainty interval.**
95% confidence intervals by cluster bootstrap over risk rows, preserving all
ratings for a sampled row within each resample, with at least 10,000 replications.

### Bayesian comparative model
For binary defect outcome `y_ij` (1 = omission, silent narrowing, orphan control,
or incorrect gate for reviewer `j` on row `i`):

```
y_ij ~ Bernoulli(p_ij)
logit(p_ij) = β₀ + β₁ · Assisted_ij + u_j + v_i
u_j ~ N(0, σ_u²),  v_i ~ N(0, σ_v²)
β₀, β₁ ~ N(0, 2.5²),  σ_u, σ_v ~ HalfNormal(0, 1)
```

Reported: posterior median of `exp(β₁)`, its 95% credible interval,
`Pr(β₁ < 0 | data)`, and posterior predicted defect rates under both conditions.
Completion time: log-normal hierarchical model with reviewer and block effects.

For isolated binomial proportions including zero-event results, a Jeffreys prior
`p ~ Beta(½, ½)` gives `p | x, n ~ Beta(x + ½, n − x + ½)`; the posterior median
and 95% credible interval are reported alongside the exact one-sided frequentist
upper bound. **Neither is described as proof of zero risk.**

Convergence requires split-R̂ < 1.01, effective sample size above 1,000 for
reported parameters, and posterior predictive checks.

## Stopping and recruitment rule (frozen)

Precision-based: minimum 8 reviewers; continue to 12 if the 95% interval for the
primary agreement estimate has half-width greater than 0.15 after 8. **This rule is
frozen before recruitment and will not be revised in response to interim data.**

## Exclusion and missing-data rules (frozen)

* A reviewer who completes fewer than 80% of assigned rows in either block is
  excluded from the paired analysis and reported descriptively.
* A row a reviewer explicitly marks "cannot determine" is coded as a non-response,
  not as a defect and not as a correct answer.
* Missing responses are reported with their exposure denominator. Krippendorff's α
  is the primary agreement statistic where missingness exceeds 5%.
* No row, reviewer or block is excluded post hoc on the basis of its result.

## Exploratory pilot (OPTIONAL, NOT REQUIRED, NOT EXECUTED)

A three-practitioner, single-pass mapping of a ~15-row held-out register may
precede the full study. Without an unaided-manual comparator it measures
feasibility and agreement — **not** silent-narrowing reduction or gate correctness
relative to manual practice. It would be labelled exploratory wherever reported,
with raw and category-specific agreement, Fleiss' κ, Krippendorff's α, Gwet's AC1,
full confusion matrices and wide uncertainty intervals disclosed. At three
reviewers and ~15 rows **no confirmatory conclusion is drawn**, and no claim in the
paper depends on it.

**This pilot has not been executed either.**

## Held-out material

The held-out expert-study register is **withheld until study close**, then released

## What would constitute a violation of this preregistration

* Reporting any `SNR` or `DF` value before the study is executed as frozen.
* Convening a panel with an authorship, ownership or commercial interest in the method.
* Modifying the held-out register, catalog or thresholds after panel review begins.
* Changing the primary outcomes, the recruitment rule, the exclusion rules or the
  priors after seeing data.
* Simulating participant responses and presenting them as empirical human evidence.

None of these has occurred, because the study has not begun.
