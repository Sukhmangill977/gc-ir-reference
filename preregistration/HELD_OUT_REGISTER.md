# Held-out expert-study register — WITHHELD UNTIL STUDY CLOSE

**This file is deliberately a placeholder. The held-out register does not exist in
this repository and must not.**

Manuscript, Data and Code Availability: *"The held-out expert-study register is
withheld until study close, then released with the study data."*

Publishing the instrument now would contaminate it: any practitioner recruited for
the RQ5 study could read the rows, the adjudicated reference, or the intended
dispositions before completing the instrument, and the study's agreement statistics
would then measure recall rather than translation quality.

## What is committed here instead

Nothing but this statement. In particular:

* **No held-out register hash is recorded.** The manuscript's Section XI-I list
  includes a "held-out-register hash", but no held-out register has been authored.
  Committing a hash of a file that does not exist, or of an empty placeholder,
  would create the appearance of a commitment that is not one. That would be worse
  than an honest gap.
* **No adjudicated reference exists**, because no adjudication panel has been
  convened (see `RQ5_DEFERRED_PROTOCOL.md`).

## What must happen before the RQ5 study begins

1. Author the held-out register: at least 30 risk rows across three domains,
   including at least one non-financial domain, with runtime-compilable,
   non-runtime, ambiguous and deliberately incomplete examples, split into two
   matched blocks per the frozen protocol.
2. Hash it and publish **only the hash**, in a new freeze
   (`preregister-tier1-v1` or later), before recruitment opens.
3. Convene the independent three-person adjudication panel.
4. Execute the study exactly as frozen in `RQ5_DEFERRED_PROTOCOL.md`.
5. Release the register itself, the raw responses and the adjudication record at
   study close.

Until step 2 has happened, the Section XI-I "held-out-register hash" item is
**openly outstanding**, and this file is the record of that.
